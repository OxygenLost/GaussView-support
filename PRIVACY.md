# GaussView Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

*Last updated: October 11, 2026 / 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日*

## English

GaussView is an offline-capable 3D Gaussian Splat file viewer for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

**Account and data collection.** GaussView does not require registration, does not contain an advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and does not operate a developer-controlled account or model-upload server. We do not automatically receive copies of models you open in the app.

**Your model files.** GaussView copies imported `.ply`, `.splat` and `.spz` files to its on-device library; it does not move or erase the originals. If iCloud Drive is enabled for GaussView, the app may synchronize library files and library metadata through Apple's iCloud Documents under your Apple Account. Apple operates that storage under its own terms and privacy policy. iCloud availability can be managed in device settings.

**Purchases.** GaussView Pro is an optional, one-time in-app purchase. Payment processing is handled by Apple. GaussView reads StoreKit-verified entitlement information to determine which features are unlocked. We do not receive payment card numbers from Apple.

**Sharing and export.** When you choose to share a model or export an image, the recipient and destination are controlled by the system sharing or save interface you use. Data you explicitly share may be handled by the recipient under their own privacy policy.

**Diagnostics.** Apple may collect and provide app analytics or crash reports according to your device's diagnostics sharing settings. GaussView does not add its own third-party tracking SDK.

**Retention and deletion.** Model files in the local library stay until you delete them or remove app data. iCloud copies are subject to your device's iCloud settings, Apple's synchronization, and its retention behavior. You may delete individual models in the app's Library. Removing the app does not necessarily delete files independently retained in iCloud.

**Support.** You can contact the developer through the [public support issue tracker](https://github.com/OxygenLost/GaussView-support/issues). All issue content is publicly visible, so please do not attach sensitive data or private model files. Any information you choose to submit there is processed by GitHub under its terms and privacy policy.

This document may change as GaussView adds features. Updates will be posted on this page.

## 简体中文

GaussView 是一款支持离线使用的 3D 高斯泼溅（Gaussian Splat）模型查看器，面向 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac。

**账号与数据收集。** GaussView 不需要注册账号，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，也没有由开发者管理的账号服务器或模型上传服务器。我们不会自动获取你在 App 中打开的模型文件。

**模型文件。** 导入 `.ply`、`.splat` 或 `.spz` 时，GaussView 会将其复制到设备本地模型库，不会移动或删除原始文件。若启用了 GaussView 的 iCloud 云盘功能，模型文件及模型库元数据可能通过 Apple iCloud 文稿同步，由你的 Apple 账户管理。iCloud 相关数据由 Apple 根据其条款与隐私政策提供服务，你可在设备设置中管理 iCloud。

**应用内购买。** GaussView Pro 是可选的一次性永久购买。Apple 负责付款处理，GaussView 只通过 StoreKit 验证的购买权益判断专业功能是否解锁。开发者无法从 Apple 获取你的银行卡号。

**分享与导出。** 只有当你选择分享模型或导出图片时，数据才会按你选择的系统分享或保存目标传递。接收方可能有独立的隐私政策。

**诊断信息。** Apple 可能根据你的设备诊断共享设置采集及提供应用分析或崩溃报告。GaussView 不内置独立的第三方追踪 SDK。

**保存与删除。** 本地模型文件会保留至你主动删除或移除 App 数据。iCloud 中的文件受设备 iCloud 设置、Apple 同步机制及其保留规则影响。你可以在 App 的模型库中删除单个模型；卸载 App 不一定会删除 iCloud 中独立保留的文件。

**联系支持。** 可通过[公开的问题反馈页面](https://github.com/OxygenLost/GaussView-support/issues)联系开发者。提交的内容任何人都可能看到，请勿公开密码、敏感资料或私人模型文件。你自愿提交到 GitHub 的内容受其条款和隐私政策约束。

若 GaussView 增加新功能，我们可能更新本政策，修订内容将公布在此页面。
